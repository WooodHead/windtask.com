This page is a sample. It exists so `/categories/` can show more than one shelf. The essay that belongs on the site is [Coding-Agent Cloud Waste](/articles/coding-agent-cloud-waste-agent-leftovers/).

Categories here are URL paths, such as `/categories/finops/` and `/categories/notes/`. They are not DNS names. `finops.windtask.com` would be a different hostname, certificate, and Worker. New topics stay in this collection until one of them needs its own origin.
